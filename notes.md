- pitch: you run a cafe, but the mugs mess with you when you look away
  - randomly adds foam
  - randomly becomes a different drink
  - randomly makes a darker/lighter roast

- drinks:
  - coffee
    - dark roast
    - medium roast
    - light roast
    - optionally with foam

- game elements:
  - customers who randomly appear to give their orders
    - patience: their time limit to deliver, shown by a little meter thing
      - idea: patience modifiers
        - add patience:
          - friendly conversation
          - regular customer
        - minus patience:
          - late for work
          - introverted
  - you can switch between two views:
    - front counter - where you take and fulfill orders
    - back counter, where your coffee stuff is
      - beans
      - grinder
      - filters (may skip)
        - have a uhhh life? dirtiness? need to replace them?
      - water
      - espresso machines
        - (may skip) they gunk up and require cleaning
          - idea: a mug which helps with that, with some other side effect?
      - mugs
        - need washing?
          - it would be _very_ weird for customers' mugs to just go right back to the mug area
            - prototype workaround: their mugs simply disappear + your cabinet manifests infinite mugs
    - 👀 stuff happens in back counter while you're looking at front counter
  - process:
    - beans -> grinder -> ground beans -> espresso machine
    - mug cabinet -> mug -> espresso machine
    - espresso machine -> coffee -> show customer view -> serve
